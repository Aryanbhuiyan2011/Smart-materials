What benefits do modern materials have?

Modern materials are very lightweight, they're engineered to be much stronger than traditional materials like steel or aluminum. They naturally can resist rust, corrison without needing any sort of protective layer.

This could be incredibly useful and save them a hell ton of money considering that so many cities spend money because of buildings getting too old, rusting out or corrosion, this is where modenr materials come in, they literally save money for cities, so the cities could actually use the money for something useful.
