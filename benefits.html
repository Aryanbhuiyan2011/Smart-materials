What benefits do smart materials have

A key benefit of smart materials is that there is no need for large motors, extra wiring or electronic sensing equipment. This also helps cut the cost of the overall product and can reduce energy consumption over time.

They can also be used in compact spaces that traditional electronics could not fit. This makes them very beneficial in a lot of areas, including medical, construction and everyday use.
