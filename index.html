Smart materials

In this project, i'll be talking about what are smart materials exactly, what can they do, how do they benefit us, what complications do they have and my opinion / reflection on it.

Overview

Smart materials are materials that can sense the changes around them and react on their own without needing any extra computers, motors or wires.

While normal smart materials like a piece of steel or plastic, just stay the same no matter what, smart materials can change their properties when they experience things like heat, pressure or electricity.
