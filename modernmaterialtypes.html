Modern material types

In this page i'll be talking about the different types of modern materials and what they exactly do.

Carbon Fiber

It's a material made out of thin strings of carbon atoms, they're tightly stuck together.

It's mainly used for aircraft wings, rocket motors and solar panel supporters.

Metallic glasses

Unlike regular metals, these sort of glasses are cooled rapidly.

They also have extreme elastic strength, they resist bending and scratching much better then steel.

Silicon carbide

It's a compound made out of silicon and carbon.

It's known as one of the most hardest synthetic materials, it can handle extreme heat and extreme cooling without cracking.

Aluminum nitride

It's a ceramic compound that is made from aluminum and nitrogen.

It's commonly used for LED's, and sometimes is used as a base for microchips.

It has high thermal conductivity, it transfers heat very easily and efficiently.
