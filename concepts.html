Some cool concepts that I came up with for smart materials

This page is just for cool concepts that I made with smart materials, they could be really useful.

The self adjusting hoodie

The strings tighten by themselves when you get hot, like you sweat, you're body heat is what provokes them.

Bendable glasses

If you sit on you're glasses they won't break, even if they fall they won't break, saves you a lot of money in the long term.

Anti slam tile

When you go to the gym and you drop a weight it makes a loud noise, these tiles makes sure it doesn't with extra padding from a smart material.
