Some cool concepts that I came up with for smart materials

This page is just for cool concepts that I made with smart materials, they could be really useful.

The self adjusting hoodie

So the reason why I created this is because I have hoodies that have drawstrings on them, and whenever I run or walk in cold weather, the strings keep on moving side to side or they get in my face and that's really annoying.

That is why I thought this would be a cool concept, It uses shape memor polymer cords and they are comfortable to wear, even when you're stretching, and they tighten whenever you're body heat gets cold or hot, so they don't move around too much.

The materials of the hoodie

I want the materials to be cotton so it'd be very comfortable when you wear it but also a blend of polyester too, when temperature regulates, and you're body is normal the polymer will loosen up and become comfortable again, it is comfortable even when it's tight but it's more comfortable when its loosened.

Automatic backpack straps

The problem is that whenever i'm carrying my bag, when I have my binder and laptop and other essentials in it it strains my shoulders and hurts.

Strats that are padded with shape memory polymers, when you load up a heavy bag, body heat and pressure makes the padding more firm to spread the weight evenly, then it softens back up when you take the bag off.

The padding makes sure that my shoulders don't strain, it also tightens to have a secure hold of the bag which is really useful when you're walking in a fast pace to get to work or school.

Bendable glasses

Ever had the problem of your glasses breaking because somebody sat on them or it just got completely demolished because it fell, well this concept is just for you.

These bendable glasses can't break, how it works is the frames have a hidden place made of shape memory metal, you can bend the glasses completely flat in half and the second you let go they snap right back to their original shape.

Most glasses actually break from this problem, and glasses aren't cheap either they're extremely expensive so this would also save a lot of money, and you wouldn't have to spend unnecessary money on buying new glasses because you broke them.

Anti slam tile

When you go to the gym, and you're chest pressing or shoulder pressing heavy weights, and you have to let it it drop to the ground after your rep you make a huge noise, these tiles are your solution.

They use recycled rubber that cushion the floor slightly, but bounce back or let heavy impacts vibrate through the concrete.

The mat is smooth and durable, it stiffens on impact and muffles noises completely when weights are dropped, which would be extremely useful considering most gyms have rules that you can't slam weights, these tiles would make it so you wouldn't even need that rule.
