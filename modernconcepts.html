Some cool concepts I came up with for Modern materials

This page will just have some ideas I came up with for modern materials

The strong jacket

It uses a specic gel insulation that make the jacket very warm even when it's cold, and it's not too thick of a jacket either, it's very comfortable.

If the jacket rips, it has its own self healing polymers that'll just close up the minor rips, it's the perfect jacket

Unbreakable case

It's made with metallic glass, it's practically unbreakable.

For the case corners, to make it fully indestructible, I used silicon carbide, so they're pretty much immune to any scratching or dropping.
