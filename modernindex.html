Overview

So you might be asking, what are modern materials? and what is the difference between smart materials and any other materials compared to modern materials, well i'm here to tell you that.

Modern materials are synthetic, heavy materials built in lats to be permanently super strong, heat resistant and ultra lightweight so they can survive extreme conditions.

The difference between modern materials and smart materials is the fact that modern materials are static, they are permanent, while smart materials as i've explained before are dynamic, and they're rather adaptive, they are not permanent at all.

Traditional materials vs Modern materials

Traditional materials are raw, they're natural and they're rarely processed, they're usually just materaisl like wood, stone clay etc. While Modern materials are synthetic, they're extremely engineered in modern labs and then are forced to basically be pushed out of their natural limits.

Traditional materials can be strong, however very heavy, and they lose their structure under intense heat, while Modern materials are able to resist extreme heat and even maintain in high strength jet engines at temperatures over 1000 degrees celcius.
